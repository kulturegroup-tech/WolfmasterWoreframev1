The team

The people behind every dog

Wolfmaster K9 is a team of trainers, decoys and handlers who work with every dog from its first weeks to the day it goes home, and long after.

Founder & head trainer

Rajender “Wolfmaster Bunty” Chauhan

Rajender has trained dogs professionally since 2002 and founded Wolfmaster K9 to bring international working-dog standards to Indian families. He is a canine behaviourist, a Protection Sports Association competitor and a PSA decoy, and he has hosted PSA trials and decoy certification camps in India.

He leads training on every dog the team places and personally oversees each handover.
