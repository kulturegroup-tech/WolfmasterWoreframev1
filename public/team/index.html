The team

The people behind every dog

Wolfmaster K9 is a team of trainers, decoys and handlers who work with every dog from its first weeks to the day it goes home, and long after.

Professional dog trainer · Champion dog handler · PSA coach · Working-dog specialist

Rajender “Wolfmaster Bunty” Chauhan

Rajender began his professional career in dog training in 2002. Over more than two decades he has built expertise across Kennel Club of India (KCI) obedience, competitive show handling, Protection Sports Association (PSA), personal protection dogs, working-dog development, scent detection and operational dog training.

His work is built on understanding each dog, recognising its potential and developing a strong partnership between dog and handler.

Two decades across every discipline

From the show ring to the trial field and operational work.

01

Competitive handling

Rajender has handled and developed numerous champion dogs in India and won multiple Best Handler awards, known for professional presentation, ring discipline and technical understanding. He has mentored many handlers who are now successful in India's dog-show community.

02

KCI obedience

He has trained German Shepherds and Labradors for KCI obedience. His red-nose American Staffordshire Terrier competed at Master Class level and, according to Rajender, earned more than 300 trophies across obedience and breed shows. He continues to coach handlers in obedience and show handling.

03

PSA since 2016

Rajender has prepared more than nine dogs for PDC and developed three dogs for PSA 1: two have earned full PSA 1 titles and the third has its first leg. He coaches obedience, engagement, control, environmental confidence and the technical demands of PSA.

04

Jade · PSA 2

His female Dutch Shepherd, Jade, has achieved a PSA 2 title and is progressing toward PSA 3. In Rajender's description, she is the only Dutch Shepherd in Asia to have achieved a PSA 2 title.

05

Trainers, handlers & decoys

Rajender develops trainers, handlers and trial decoys. His coaching covers training mechanics, handling skills, decoy development, timing, movement and safety. One of his student decoys has been recognised as one of Asia's youngest.

06

Working & operational dogs

He has worked with companies, government-sector organisations and military personnel on patrol dog training, scent-work and detection, explosive-detection dog training, protection-work development, handler education and environmental conditioning.

07

Personal protection dogs

His approach to protection dogs focuses on stability, obedience, impulse control and appropriate protective responses rather than indiscriminate aggression. Trained dogs are offered for personal protection, property security and specialised working roles, according to each dog's suitability and training.

08

Training professionals

Rajender trains professionals across India in PSA, obedience, personal protection and working dogs, teaching them to understand behaviour, evaluate performance and make sound training decisions.

Build better dogs. Develop better handlers. Train better trainers.

Raise the standard of the canine industry. That is the mission behind every dog, handler and trainer Wolfmaster K9 works with.
