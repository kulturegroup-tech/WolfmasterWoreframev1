FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is a protection dog safe around children?+

A well-selected, properly trained protection dog is calm and social at home. We select for stable temperament first and test every family dog around children before recommending it.

Will the dog listen to me, or only to the trainer?+

Handover includes handler training until the dog responds to you reliably. We keep supporting you afterwards.

Can you train the dog I already have?+

Often, yes. We assess your dog's temperament and health first, then recommend obedience, behaviour or protection training as suitable.

How much does a protection dog cost?+

It depends on the dog, its level of training and the work you need. We share prices after a consultation. Our guide explains what goes into the cost.

Where are you based?+

We are based in Faridabad, Delhi NCR and work with clients across the NCR, and further across India on request.

What happens after the dog comes home?+

Home integration sessions, scheduled check-ins and lifetime support from the team.
