Board & train

Board & train programs

Your dog lives and trains with the Wolfmaster team, with structured sessions every day. When it comes home, you learn to handle it with the same results.

Book an assessment+91 98733 35698

Choose a program

Every program starts with an assessment. We recommend the program and its length once we have met your dog.

Obedience board & train

Reliable obedience and off-leash control: heel, recall, place, stays and calm manners around people, traffic and other dogs.

Behaviour board & train

For dogs with reactivity, fear or aggression. A structured behaviour plan worked daily by an experienced behaviourist.

Protection board & train

For suitable dogs: protection work built on obedience and a clear off switch, proofed in real environments.

Puppy board & train

Foundation training for young dogs: socialisation, confidence, engagement and early obedience.

What every program includes

Your dog follows the same daily routine as the dogs we train for placement, with progress updates from the team throughout.

  • Assessment before the program starts
  • Daily structured training sessions
  • Exercise, feeding and care at our facility
  • Regular photo and video progress updates
  • Handler sessions so you get the same results
  • Follow-up support once your dog is home
  1. 1

    Assessment

    We meet your dog, understand the problem or goal and recommend a program.

  2. 2

    Arrival

    Your dog settles in with the team and its daily routine begins.

  3. 3

    Training

    Daily sessions, with regular updates so you can follow its progress.

  4. 4

    Handover

    You train with your dog and our trainers before it goes home.

  5. 5

    Follow-up

    Check-ins and refresher sessions to keep the results.
